FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers before you take the next step

What training areas are associated with Amreen's Academy?＋

The academy's Instagram bio describes makeup, mehendi and beautician services. Its homepage also presents Professional Makeup Artistry, Skin & Aesthetics, Hair Styling and Bridal Artistry.

Does the academy offer a 45-day course?＋

The academy homepage features a 45-Day Beauty & Makeup Course. Contact the academy to confirm the current batch, syllabus, schedule and availability.

What are the fees and batch dates?＋

The full course fee is ₹25,000. A three-instalment payment option is available. Confirm instalment amounts, due dates and any applicable terms directly with the academy.

Do all courses run for 45 days?＋

The four areas shown on the website are included within one integrated 45-day Beauty & Makeup course: Professional Makeup Artistry, Skin & Aesthetics, Hair Styling and Bridal Artistry. They are not four separately priced courses.

Can I pay in three instalments?＋

Yes. A three-instalment payment option is available for the ₹25,000 course. Confirm the instalment amounts and due dates directly with the academy.

Is a certificate included?＋

The academy provides a course certificate on successful completion of the 45-day integrated program. Confirm assessment and completion requirements before enrolling. No external accreditation is claimed on this website.

The journey at Amreen Academy

01

First, the foundation.

Skin anatomy, hygiene, tools and product knowledge. Before you touch a brush or a blade you learn why skin behaves the way it does, so every service you give later is safe and confident.

  • Skin types
  • Hygiene and safety
  • Tools and products
02

Then, your hands learn.

Practice on mannequins first, then on live models under Amreen's guidance. Cleanups, threading, waxing, blow-dry and base makeup, repeated until they feel natural.

  • Live-model practicals
  • Small batches
  • Individual feedback
03

Next, the signature crafts.

Bridal and airbrush makeup, hair colour and keratin chemistry, gel and acrylic nails, saree draping. The services clients pay a premium for, taught step by step.

  • Bridal and airbrush
  • Colour and keratin
  • Nail extensions
04

Finally, your own studio.

You leave with a certificate, a model portfolio shoot and your own starter kit. Whether you join a salon, freelance for brides or open your own space, you start with proof of your work.

  • Certificate
  • Portfolio shoot
  • Starter kit

Complete salon training

Every service a salon offers, you learn here.

Pick one craft or build the full skill set. Each track has its own practical sessions on live models.

Makeup artistry

Skin prep, colour theory, HD base, party, bridal and airbrush finishes.

Hair styling

Blow-dry, braids, buns and bridal hairstyles with accessory pinning.

Hair colour and chemical

Trichology, bond repair, keratin, cysteine, balayage and developer volumes.

Skin and facials

Facial massage, cleanups, extraction hygiene and hydra-facial style protocols.

Nail art

Acrylic and gel extensions, e-file technique, chrome, cat-eye and crystal art.

Waxing and threading

Rica waxing technique, threading, hygiene and aftercare for every skin type.

Saree and dupatta draping

South Indian, north Indian and lehenga draping styles for bridal clients.

Client and business skills

Consultations, pricing, social media branding and building your portfolio.

Questions, answered.

Do I get tools and products during training?

Yes. Students receive a professional starter kit to keep, including brushes, practice palettes and tools.

Can I learn just one skill, like nails or hair colour?

Yes. Each craft has its own course, or you can combine them for a full salon skill set.

How big are the batches?

Batches are kept small, up to ten students, so every learner gets individual attention.

Is the certificate recognised?

Graduates receive an ISO-recognised certificate. Ask the academy for the certificate details when you enquire.

Do you take bridal bookings too?

Yes. The Makeover Studio handles bridal and event bookings across Bengaluru. Message us with your date.
